How to claim

New to this? Here's the whole process in plain English. It usually takes about 15 minutes per year you're claiming for.

Find your PPS number

It's on your payslip, your Public Services Card, or any letter from Revenue or Social Welfare. It looks like 1234567AB.

Get into myAccount

myAccount is Revenue's free website for PAYE workers. If you have a verified MyGovID, log in with that. Otherwise, register with your PPS number, date of birth, phone, email and address. If you can give two of these (your driving licence number, details from a payslip, or details from a tax assessment), you get in straight away. If not, Revenue posts you a password.

Register for myAccount on Revenue.ie

Gather your paperwork

Receipts, rent details, bills. Your results list exactly what each credit needs. You don't send receipts in. Keep them for six years in case Revenue asks.

Claim past years

For 2022 to 2025, use Review your tax for the previous 4 years. Pick a year, request a Statement of Liability (a summary of what you earned and paid), then fill in a short Income Tax Return and add your credits. Do one year at a time.

Claim this year

For 2026, use Manage your tax for the current year. The credit is usually applied through your wages, so you see it in your pay instead of as a lump sum.

Get the refund

Refunds for past years go into your bank account. Revenue says most online health expense claims are refunded within five working days. Check your bank details are up to date in myAccount first.

Watch out for scams. Fake "Revenue refund" texts and emails are common. Revenue says it never emails asking for personal information. Always type revenue.ie into your browser yourself instead of clicking links in messages, and never share your myAccount password with anyone.

Words you'll see, explained

PAYE
Pay As You Earn. Your employer takes tax from your wages each payday. If you have a normal job, you're PAYE.
Tax credit
An amount taken straight off the tax you owe. A €1,000 credit means €1,000 less tax.
Tax relief
Money taken off your income before tax is worked out. It saves you a percentage of the amount: usually 20%, or 40% if you pay the higher rate.
Statement of Liability
Revenue's end-of-year summary of what you earned, paid and owed. You request it in myAccount before claiming for a past year.
Emergency tax
A higher rate of tax your employer has to use when they don't have your tax details yet, for example in a new job.
Jointly assessed
Married couples and civil partners can be taxed together. Some credits double for jointly assessed couples, and unused credits can move between you.
RTB number
Your landlord's registration number for the tenancy with the Residential Tenancies Board. Ask your landlord or check your lease.
Four-year rule
You can only claim refunds for the last four years. Right now that's 2022 to 2025, plus the current year.

Common questions

What if Revenue says I underpaid tax?

When you open a past year, your Statement of Liability can occasionally show you paid too little, for example because of a second job or a taxable social welfare payment. Adding the credits you're owed can reduce or cancel it out.

If you still owe under €6,000, you can pay it in myAccount, or let Revenue collect it interest-free by reducing your tax credits over up to four years. Over €6,000, Revenue sends a notice and you can contact them through MyEnquiries to agree how to pay.

What to do if you have underpaid (Revenue.ie)

Will claiming get me audited?

Claiming credits you're entitled to is completely normal, and hundreds of thousands of people do it every year. Revenue does check some claims and may ask to see your receipts, so keep them for six years. As long as you only claim what you actually paid for, there's nothing to worry about.

Do I need my landlord's permission to claim the rent credit?

No. You claim it yourself, and you don't need your landlord to do anything. You'll be asked for their name, the address and how much rent you paid. The one condition is that you're renting with the landlord's agreement, so an unofficial sublet doesn't count.

What if my landlord hasn't registered with the RTB?

You can still claim without an RTB number, but Revenue may ask for it later and could take the credit back if you can't give it. Some types of tenancy, such as student accommodation from colleges and private providers, must be registered or you can't claim. Rooms in someone's home (rent-a-room or digs) don't need to be registered and still count.

Rent Tax Credit conditions (Revenue.ie)

I've left Ireland. Can I still claim?

Yes. If you worked here for part of the year before moving abroad, you may be due a refund. In myAccount, go to PAYE Services and choose Claim unemployment repayment. Credits for past years are claimed the usual way, through Review your tax for the previous 4 years.

Not online? Send Form P50, a Form 12, and a short statement that you've moved abroad permanently (or won't be Irish resident next tax year) to your Revenue office.

Refunds when leaving Ireland (Revenue.ie)

Do I have to pay someone to do this?

No. myAccount is free, and every claim on this site can be done yourself. Tax refund agents usually take a cut of your refund for the same steps.
